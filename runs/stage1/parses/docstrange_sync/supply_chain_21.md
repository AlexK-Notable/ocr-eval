In 1, enter the name of the court document you are sending to the other parties in the court case (e.g., a Court Order or Answer).

In 2, enter the date you send this form to the other parties. You should send this form by 5:00 p.m. on the same day it was filed with the Circuit Clerk.

In 3, enter the full name and address of the parties or lawyers to whom you are sending a copy of this form, and check if you are sending copies of this form by hand delivery, by mail, or by email.

If a party has a lawyer, you must send a copy of this form to the lawyer.

**CAUTION:** You may only send this form by email if the other party has agreed to receive documents in the lawsuit by email.
