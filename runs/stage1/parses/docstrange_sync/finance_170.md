Stock Awards
Stock awards were granted under the Incentive Plan in September 2024 for shares of Microsoft common stock to Named Executives other than Mr. Nadella. These stock awards vest over four years, with 25% vesting on August 31, 2025 and 12.5% each six-months thereafter, to support long-term focus and align with shareholders' interests as the value of such awards is dependent on our stock price and vesting is subject to continued employment, except as described on page 47 below.

No Other Fiscal Year 2025 Compensation
During fiscal year 2025, the Compensation Committee and independent members of our Board awarded no other compensation to our Named Executives.
