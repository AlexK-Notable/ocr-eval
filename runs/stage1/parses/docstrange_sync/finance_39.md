**Figure 7 – Inspections Relative to Audit Report Date (Days Between Report Date and Fieldwork)**

&lt;img&gt;Bar chart showing the distribution of inspection start times relative to the auditor's report date for 2021, 2022, and 2023. The x-axis represents the percentage of inspections, and the y-axis shows the number of days between the report date and the first day of fieldwork. The chart is divided into seven categories: >360, 301 to 360, 241 to 300, 211 to 240, 151 to 210, 91 to 150, and <90. Each category has three horizontal bars representing the years 2021 (light blue), 2022 (medium blue), and 2023 (dark blue). The percentages for each bar are labeled at the end of the bar.&lt;/img&gt;

A complete and final set of audit documentation should be assembled for retention as of a date not more than 45 days after the report release date (documentation completion date). Not all firms use the full 45-day period and therefore, in those cases, our inspections can start as soon as the firm informs us the final set of audit documentation has been assembled for retention. Most inspections are performed within seven months of the report release date. Inspections performed after 301 days are mostly related to non-U.S. firms with only one public company audit or our decision to select a prior period audit to be unpredictable.
