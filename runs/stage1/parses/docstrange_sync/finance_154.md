SNAP Data

In fiscal year (FY) 2024, 53 State agencies participated in SNAP which provided more than $93 billion in program benefits through an EBT system to SNAP households.¹⁰ In addition, FNS authorized more than 260,000 retailers to accept SNAP benefits in exchange for eligible grocery items purchased by more than 22 million households in FY 2024. SNAP benefit costs and household participation have fluctuated since FY 2020, as depicted in Figure 5 below.¹¹
