# Target Team Focus in 2023

In 2023, the PCAOB’s target team, a group of inspectors who focus on emerging audit risks and issues, reviewed procedures on selected audits of identified public companies with material crypto assets, multi-location audits, interim reviews in support of Form 10-Q filings of 10 banks, and public companies that had a significant unusual transaction or event. Observations of those inspections will be published in a future Spotlight.

In December 2023 we published our Spotlight, “Observations From the Target Team’s 2022 Inspections.” This Spotlight highlights the target team’s 2022 inspections.
