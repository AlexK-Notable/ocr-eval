# County of Los Angeles, Department of Coroner
## Investigator's Narrative

Case Number: 2020-05405
Decedent: GUARDADO PINEDA, ANDRES F.

---

## Evidence:

Physical evidence was collected. On 06/19/2020 at 0255 hours, I utilized a GSR kit on the decedent's face and both his hands at the scene. Then at 0415 hours, I removed and collected latex gloves off both the decedent's hands at the Decedent Services Unit. Then at 0420 hours, I utilized a fingernail kit to swab underneath all of the decedent's fingernails at the Decedent Services Unit. Then at 0444 hours, I utilized a hair kit to collect hair strands from the decedent's head at the Decedent Services Unit. I later booked the GSR kit, the gloves, the hair kit, and the fingernail kit as evidence at the Forensic Science Center.

---

## Body Examination:

The decedent is an 18-year-old Hispanic male with brown eyes, brown hair, natural teeth, and a mustache. No scars or tattoos were noted. The decedent was wearing black latex gloves, black sunglasses on the back of his head, black pants, gray underwear, black socks, and white shoes. His pants had been cut by Paramedics and his shirt was collected as evidence by the Police Criminalist on scene. He was seen lying supine on the ground. The top of his head was facing west with both of his arms resting by his sides. Both of his legs were straight, with both of his feet pointing east.

His left eye appeared to be congested and bloodshot. There was dry blood residue noted to his head. There was a small abrasion noted to his left upper forehead. There was an apparent gunshot wound to his right clavicle area. There was an apparent gunshot wound to his upper right chest. There was an apparent gunshot wound to the left side of his middle abdomen. There was an apparent graze wound to his left inner forearm, his right inner forearm, and his left middle abdomen. There were a total of 5 apparent gunshot wounds noted to his upper, middle, and lower backside. Rigor mortis was absent. Lividity was light in color, seen to his backside, and blanched with pressure.

---
