# MSQ- MEDICAL SYMPTOM/TOXICITY QUESTIONNAIRE

The Toxicity and Symptom Screening Questionnaire identifies symptoms that help to identify the underlying causes of illness, and helps you track your progress over time. Rate each of the following symptoms based upon your health profile. If you are taking this questionnaire for the first time, record your symptoms for the last 48 hours ONLY. If this is a follow-up questionnaire, record your symptoms for the past 30 days.

# POINT SCALE

0= Never or almost never have the symptom

2= Occasionally have it, effect is severe

4= Frequently have it, effect is severe

1= Occasionally have it, effect is not severe

3= Frequently have it, effect is not severe
