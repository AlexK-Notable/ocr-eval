8. BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES

Disaster Recovery Homebuyer Assistance Program (DR-HBA) Projects are funded to assist with housing needs of low-to moderate income (LMI) households and eligibility is limited to residents of Most Impacted and Distressed (MID) California counties that are also eligible to receive housing assistance through the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Individual Assistance pursuant to the 2018 Federal Disaster Declarations DR-4382 and DR-4407 and 2020 Federal Disaster Declarations DR-4558 and DR-4569. The HBA Program will provide down payment assistance to eligible applicants to cover the gap in affordability of acquiring a new primary residence in a non-high or very high fire hazard severity zone throughout California. The HBA program will allow applicants to identify and select properties which meets the The U.S Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) minimum property standard based on household size and work with first lender for financing options.

9. AGREEMENT OUTLINE (Include reason for Agreement: Identify specific problem, administrative requirement, program need or other circumstances making the Agreement necessary; include special or unusual terms and conditions.)

Golden State Finance Authority (GSFA) will act as a subrecipient and will facilitate the development of a downpayment assistance program for single family housing and perform the following tasks pursuant to the Disaster Recovery Homebuyer Assistance Program (CDBG-DR-HBA):

- Develop education and outreach campaign for lenders, realtors, and homebuyers.
- Process application for eligibility, underwriting and closing home loans in compliance with consumer lending requirements with direct lenders and in accordance with the DR-HBA Policies and Procedures.
- Develop HBA loan documents, policies and procedures, standard operating procedures.
- Handle federal reporting and 5-year monitoring and compliance requirements
